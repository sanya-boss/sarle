About us

Located at Aia tn 17, amid the historic architecture of the Old Town, the gallery creates a space where art and people meet.

Exhibitions, concerts, auctions, lectures, meetings with artists, educational and charitable projects take place here. It is a place where art does not exist separately from life — it becomes a part of it.

CURRENT EXHIBITION

CONTINUATION
OF THE BEGINNING

Evgeny Kos solo exhibition continues an artistic journey begun earlier. Nature, dreamlike imagery and the mechanical world meet in surreal works. The railway becomes a symbol of movement, inner searching and change. Each canvas creates a space for sincere dialogue with the viewer. The exhibition invites us to pause, look closer and feel life’s movement.
