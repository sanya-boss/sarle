About us

Located at Aia tn 17, amid the historic architecture of the Old Town, the gallery creates a space where art and people meet.

Exhibitions, concerts, auctions, lectures, meetings with artists, educational and charitable projects take place here. It is a place where art does not exist separately from life — it becomes a part of it.

OPEN CALL 2027: INTERNATIONAL ARTIST SELECTION

YOUR ART
DESERVES TO BE SEEN.

Sarle Art Gallery & Studio, a contemporary art gallery located in the historic heart of Tallinn, Estonia, invites artists from around the world to apply for OPEN CALL 2027.

We are seeking emerging and established artists with unique perspectives, distinctive artistic practices, and a passion for contemporary art. Our mission is to discover new artistic voices, create meaningful connections, and develop international opportunities for artists.

This is more than an open call for an exhibition. Selected artists will be considered for participation in upcoming exhibitions, international art projects, and potential long-term collaboration with Sarle Art Gallery.

How it works

All applications will be carefully reviewed by the Sarle Art Gallery team. Artists will be selected based on the quality of their work, artistic vision, originality, and relevance to upcoming gallery projects.

Selected artists may be invited to participate in exhibitions and other gallery initiatives. A limited number of artists may receive fully supported participation opportunities. Additional selected artists may be offered individual participation arrangements, depending on the exhibition format and available opportunities.

Applications are free to submit. Participation conditions will be communicated individually to selected applicants before any commitment is made.

Submitting an application does not guarantee exhibition participation or gallery representation. Artists whose work resonates with our curatorial vision may also be considered for future exhibitions and long-term collaboration.
