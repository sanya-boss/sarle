GALLERY

Founded by Svetlana Sarle

In memory of Vilnis Strazdinš

Aia tn 17, Old Town

Estonia, Tallinn, the Old Town — the heart of its people, and it is here, at Aia tn 17, that Sarle Art Gallery & Studio is located.

A contemporary artistic and cultural space that has organically become part of the historical environment of the Old Town.

At the same address, you will also find the gallery of renowned Estonian sculptor Tauno Kangro. The coexistence of different artistic directions creates a unique atmosphere — a space of dialogue between tradition and contemporary art.

Today, Sarle Art Gallery & Studio brings together painting, graphics, photography, sculpture, installation, multimedia art, music, and educational projects.

Gallery

VILNIS
STRAZDINŠ

Sarle Art Gallery & Studio was founded by Svetlana Sarle in memory of her close friend Vilnis Strazdinš, who first envisioned the idea of opening a gallery.

Vilnis Strazdinš was a prominent Latvian businessman, yet deeply passionate about painting, music, and the dream of having his own gallery.

In Tallinn, Vilnis opened a clinic and, seeing people struggling with illness, said:

«We must distract them from their suffering and create a project where, by spending a few hours in a hall, people can immerse themselves in healthy beauty and classical music.»

— Vilnis Strazdinš

Art enthusiast

His dream was for the gallery to have a dress code — and that dream has come true.

At 63, he passed away, leaving behind bright, warm, heartfelt memories. He had another wish: to help parents in raising their children. He was genuinely happy when speaking about his little friend with the unusual name Sky.

“Heavenly beings,” — Vilnis would say, — “must be untouchable and surrounded by music and love.”

— Vilnis Strazdinš

His dream has come true: the temple of art is alive.

In one year, it has hosted 13 exhibitions, 7 concerts, and numerous lectures.

Thank you, Vilnis Strazdinš.

Tallinn will preserve your memory.

The founder

SVETLANA
SARLE

Behind Sarle Art Gallery & Studio stands a person for whom art is inseparable from human feelings, relationships, and inner honesty.

Svetlana Sarle possesses a rare combination of strength and sensitivity. Those who know her closely speak of her openness, remarkable emotional depth, kindness, and her ability to empathize profoundly with others.

Her character unites an inner core with a warm and open attitude toward people. Despite disappointments and life’s challenges, she has preserved her ability to believe in others, to remain honest, decent, and deeply compassionate.

For Svetlana, creating the gallery became not only the fulfillment of her close friend Vilnis Strazdinš’s dream, but also a continuation of her own values — love for people and beauty, respect for memory, gratitude, and the desire to create.

Svetlana is a trained dramatic theatre actress. She worked in theatre and as a television announcer. Later, she led a clinic and, after completing that chapter of her life, returned once again to creativity.

Sarle Art Gallery & Studio was born from the desire to create a space where art remains genuine, and where a person can remain themselves.

A cultural space in the very heart of Old Tallinn, bringing together art, music, exhibitions, and creative events.
